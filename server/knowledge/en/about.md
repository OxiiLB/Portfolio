## Who he is

Romain Giraud is a freelance web and AI developer based in France. He helps small businesses, agencies and solo entrepreneurs save time with AI. He works fully remotely with clients worldwide and adapts to European, American and Asian time zones.

## What he offers

- AI assistants and chatbots (RAG): customer support, internal FAQ or knowledge base powered by the client's own documents.
- Automation: data extraction and structuring, follow-ups, workflows connecting the client's tools and services.
- AI integration: adding AI features to an existing website, app or software product.
- Web development: custom websites and web apps (Vue.js, Nuxt, Angular), migration and optimization of existing code.

## Freelance work (since September 2026)

Since September 2026 Romain has worked as a freelance developer specialised in AI integration. He has designed RAG document assistants for small businesses and agencies, built data extraction and structuring agents, and integrated AI features into existing products. For AI image generation, his pipeline produces one visual in about 40 seconds, versus several hours of manual retouching.
