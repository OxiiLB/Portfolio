Clients can book Romain on Malt, Codeur.com or Fiverr, or contact him directly by email. Links are at the bottom of the website, in the green contact block (the "Hire me" button leads there).

For a project that doesn't fit a package, the best first step is to send a short description, and Romain replies with a quote.
